Meet Ryan Ellison, RPT

I've spent more than ten years working on pianos for a living, including Yamaha grands and uprights, Disklavier, Silent and Hybrid pianos, and Bösendorfer. I started Ryan Ellison Piano to give local piano owners the same careful, technical service on every instrument, from a family upright to a high-end grand.

Specialties: Yamaha grand and upright pianos, Disklavier, Silent and Hybrid pianos, and Bösendorfer pianos.

Training and credentials

  • Registered Piano Technician, Piano Technicians Guild (2017)
  • Grand Piano Master Course, Yamaha Piano Technical Academy, Japan (2019)
  • Grand Piano Course, Yamaha Piano Technical Academy, Japan (2017)
  • Chicago School for Piano Technology (2011)
  • Bachelor of Music (2007)

What I care about: A piano that holds its tuning, feels even under the fingers, and sounds the way it should. I'll tell you plainly what your piano needs and what it doesn't.
