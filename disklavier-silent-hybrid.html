Service for Disklavier, Silent and Hybrid pianos

These pianos add sensors, solenoids and electronics to an acoustic piano, and many tuners avoid them. After ten years with Yamaha pianos, this is what I do every day.

What D.S.H. Maintenance includes ($179)

  • Calibration and table updates for the Disklavier, Silent or Hybrid system
  • Adjustments so playback, recording and silent mode work as they should
  • A check of the connected hardware

Repairs start at $79. For bigger electronic or mechanical problems, I quote before I start.

Tuning is separate. Most people book an ear tuning with D.S.H. Maintenance in the same visit. D.S.H. Maintenance is paid in full by credit card when you book.
