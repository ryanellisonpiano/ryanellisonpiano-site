Service for Disklavier, Silent and Hybrid pianos

These pianos combine an acoustic piano with sensors, solenoids and electronics. Many tuners are not comfortable working on them. After ten years working on Yamaha pianos, this is what I do every day.

What is included in D.S.H. Maintenance ($179)

  • Calibration and table updates for the Disklavier, Silent or Hybrid system
  • Adjustments to the system so playback, recording and silent mode respond correctly
  • A check of the connected hardware that the piano depends on

Repairs start at $79. Larger electronic or mechanical problems are quoted before work begins.

Tuning is billed separately. Most owners book an ear tuning and D.S.H. Maintenance in the same visit, so the piano sounds right and plays right. D.S.H. Maintenance is paid in full by credit card when you book.

Not sure what your piano needs? Call (657) 438-4984 or book online and describe the model. I will tell you what to expect before I arrive.
