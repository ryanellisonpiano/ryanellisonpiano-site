Service for Disklavier, Silent and Hybrid pianos

A Disklavier, Silent or Hybrid piano is an acoustic piano with sensors, solenoids and electronics built in, and a lot of tuners aren't comfortable working on them. After ten years with Yamaha pianos, this is what I do every day.

What is included in D.S.H. Maintenance ($179)

  • Calibration and table updates for the Disklavier, Silent or Hybrid system
  • Adjustments so playback, recording and silent mode respond the way they should
  • A check of the connected hardware the piano relies on

Repairs start at $79. For bigger electronic or mechanical problems, I'll give you a quote before I start.

Tuning is a separate charge. Most people book an ear tuning along with D.S.H. Maintenance in the same visit, so the piano sounds right and plays right. D.S.H. Maintenance is paid in full by credit card when you book.

Not sure what your piano needs? Call me at (657) 438-4984 or book online and tell me the model. I'll let you know what to expect before I arrive.
