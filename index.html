88 keys, 88 constellations

The 88 piano keys make music.

Neil deGrasse Tyson, "Menagerie" (1994)

A piano has 88 keys. The night sky has 88 official constellations. Tyson sets them side by side in that essay, and jokes that the sky's side is more of a zoo. In 1619 Johannes Kepler tried something more musical. His book Harmonices Mundi, "The Harmony of the World," argued the planets moved in musical ratios. He never proved the planets sing, but that same book gave us his third law of planetary motion.

I tune pianos for a living, so I like the idea. Every piano I work on is my small attempt at harmony.
